I am a Robotics AI Engineer at <a href='https://research.samsung.com/'>Samsung Research</a>, where I focus on learning-based dexterous robot manipulation. My research interests include Imitation Learning (IL) and Reinforcement Learning (RL) for robot manipulation and humanoids.

At Samsung Research, I spent most of my time developing Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models and Behavior Cloning (BC) robotic policies for precise robotic tasks. I also contributed to Sound Source Localization (SSL) for <a href='https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YBfSX3QiqDM/'>Samsung Ballie</a>.

Previously: I worked as a software intern at <a href='https://www.bearrobotics.ai/'>Bear Robotics</a>. I obtained my Bachelor’s degree in Mechanical Engineering from Yonsei University.

Taehan Kim

Robotics AI Engineer @ Samsung Research.

I am a Robotics AI Engineer at Samsung Research, where I focus on learning-based dexterous robot manipulation. My research interests include Imitation Learning (IL) and Reinforcement Learning (RL) for robot manipulation and humanoids.

At Samsung Research, I spent most of my time developing Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models and Behavior Cloning (BC) robotic policies for precise robotic tasks. I also contributed to Sound Source Localization (SSL) for Samsung Ballie.

Previously: I worked as a software intern at Bear Robotics. I obtained my Bachelor’s degree in Mechanical Engineering from Yonsei University.

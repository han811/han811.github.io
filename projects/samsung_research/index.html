Samsung Research

As a Robotics AI Engineer at Samsung Research, I conducted research on imitation learning and reinforcement learning for robot manipulation and humanoids. I also developed a sound source localization algorithm for Samsung Ballie.

Period: 2022.07 - present


Projects

My work at Samsung Research focuses on two main areas:

  1. Developing imitation learning models (e.g., ACT, Diffusion Policy) for robot manipulation and humanoids.
  2. Applying reinforcement learning to fine-tune these models for precise tasks.

My work on the imitation learning project for robot policy involved designing a model architecture that uses FT (Force/Torque) sensors for high-precision tasks. To improve performance, I integrated various vision backbones to leverage spatial intelligence and developed algorithms to automate the data acquisition process. (My work was based on OCTO)

In the reinforcement learning fine-tuning project, I worked on training the residuals of pre-trained imitation learning and VLA (Vision-Language-Action) models, which enabled the application of learning-based algorithms for precision tasks. Furthermore, my research involved an approach of training a relatively small reinforcement learning policy with offline RL and then fine-tuning it with online RL. (My work was based on paper1 and paper2)

Additionally, I also have experience developing a light-weight Sound Source Localization (SSL) algorithm for the robot, Samsung Ballie.

This project focused on developing an AI model to seamlessly remove unwanted objects from videos. The process involved three stages: Video Object Segmentation, Video Inpainting, and assigning new imagery to the movements of the inpainting object. Each stage was powered by a specific deep learning model: VOSNet, VINet, and AANet, respectively. My role was to implement the VINet model to perform the video inpainting task.

**Period: 2021.03 - 2021.07**

The [RoboMaster AI Challenge](https://www.robomaster.com/en-US/) is an international robotics competition for university teams, held at ICRA. As **captain** of **Roboin**, [Yonsei University's representative team](/assets/pdf/support_letter.pdf), I led the software development for our robots.

- Developed the **autonomous navigation stack**, optimizing the Dynamic Window Approach (DWA) for path planning and integrating **YOLOv3** for real-time perception.
- Built gimbal aiming control with PID and designed behavior policies using **reinforcement learning (DQN, DDPG)** and decision trees for collaborative multi-robot behavior in simulation.
- Our [technical report](/assets/pdf/Roboin_Technical_Report.pdf) received an [S+ rank](https://www.robomaster.com/en-US/resource/pages/announcement/1020) (top 4 of 68 universities) and the team won [3rd prize](https://www.robomaster.com/en-US/resource/pages/announcement/1027) in the finals among 39 teams.

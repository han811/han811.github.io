The <a href='https://www.robomaster.com/en-US/'>RoboMaster AI Challenge</a> is a prestigious international robotics competition for university teams. As the **captain** of **"Roboin"**, <a href='/assets/pdf/support_letter.pdf'>my university's representative team</a>, I led the software development for our robots. My key contributions included real-time object detection using YOLO V3, gimbal aiming control with PID, ROS-based navigation algorithms, and designing behavior policies through reinforcement learning and decision trees. Our team's efforts culminated in <a href='https://www.robomaster.com/en-US/resource/pages/announcement/1020'>an S grade</a> on <a href='/assets/pdf/Roboin_Technical_Report.pdf'>the final technical report</a> and <a href='https://www.robomaster.com/en-US/resource/pages/announcement/1027'>a 3rd prize</a> in the finals.

**Period: 2018.12 - 2019.05**
